Rotation Rescue: assumptions & evidence

NOT A FARMING RECOMMENDATION. All crop water-demand values, soil coefficients, crop-cover values and crop-diversity threshold are provisional screening rules. A passing result proves only internal mathematical feasibility under these assumptions. No yields, water savings or soil-health improvements have been validated.

What is computed?

Each historical scenario reuses that one observed year’s Kharif and Rabi totals across both years of the modeled plan; it is not a consecutive two-year climate replay. A four-season sequence (Kharif, Rabi, Kharif, Rabi) is evaluated against a simplified per-slot water budget. All 256 plans from an eight-crop catalog are enumerated. The model chooses the passing plan with the fewest crop substitutions; ties favor crop-family diversity, indicative cover days, then lower total assumed water demand.

Equation and input ownership

Modeled available water (mm) = seasonal rainfall (mm) × soil utilization factor × scenario multiplier + user-input irrigation (mm per season).
Modeled deficit = max(0, crop's assumed demand − modeled available water). Available water is rounded to the nearest whole mm before comparison. This introduces threshold sensitivity: in the illustrative Chickpea example the unrounded break-even Rabi rainfall is 133.33 mm, while the implemented rounding lets 133 mm pass. The model does not simulate runoff, evapotranspiration, storage or actual crop-stage water balance.
