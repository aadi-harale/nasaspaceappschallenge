The problem

Farmers cannot simply restart every season.

Rainfall variability and limited irrigation can disrupt familiar crop sequences. A replacement plan must respect practical constraints and consider soil-protection practices, not just recommend a different crop.

Our contribution

Repair instead of replace.

We enumerate 256 four-season crop combinations from an eight-crop illustrative catalog and select the feasible candidate requiring the fewest crop substitutions.

Optimality applies only to the encoded candidate set and simplified water/diversity constraints.

Evidence flow

NASA observations → explainable decision

01NASA POWER daily regional precipitation
02Seasonal aggregation + farmer assumptions
03Water-budget failures + minimal repair

NASA POWER data are regional context, not field-level soil measurements. Live browser data retrieval still requires independent verification.

Prototype workflow

Why it matters

Adaptation that respects the farmer.

AgroShift turns a vague climate warning into a traceable decision: what fails, what can change, and what remains uncertain.
